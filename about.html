Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small neighborhood business created to bring fresh, handmade breads and pastries to local families, commuters, and community organizations.

What started with a few daily loaves has grown into a welcoming bakery that also prepares pastries, cookies, celebration cakes, and advance orders for special events.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We select ingredients based on freshness, quality, and responsible sourcing. Whenever possible, we work with nearby suppliers and choose seasonal ingredients.

  • Flour selected for quality and consistent baking
  • Seasonal fruits purchased from regional suppliers
  • Fresh dairy products used in pastries and cakes
  • Small-batch preparation to reduce unnecessary waste

Customers with food allergies should contact the bakery before ordering so our team can discuss ingredients and preparation practices.
